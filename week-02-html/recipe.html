Ingredients

  • Beef tenderloin
  • Oyster sauce and Soy sauce
  • Palm sugar and Black pepper
  • Garlic
  • Fresh lettuce, sliced tomatoes, and cucumbers
  • Lime juice and salt

Steps

  1. Marinate: Mix the beef with oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar, and black pepper. Let it sit for 20 minutes.
  2. Prepare the Platter: Arrange a bed of fresh lettuce, tomatoes, and onions on a plate.
  3. Stir-fry: Heat oil in a wok over high heat. Add garlic, then sear the beef quickly until browned but juicy.
  4. Dipping Sauce: Mix lime juice, salt, and plenty of black pepper in a small bowl.
  5. Serve: Place the hot beef over the vegetables and serve with steamed rice or a fried egg.
